      Bump Chart

      2 cardsHeight 2 units

      Ranks per period joined with S-curves, to show how the order changed rather than the values. Rank 1 is at the top.

      Chart guide · Bump chart

      Bump - Basic

      bump-basic

      A card that shows how several items' ranks changed from period to period, all at once. Use it to show a ranking race between branches or products.

      Judgment built in (3)
      • Give values (values) and each period is ranked from largest to smallest. Ranks hide the size of the differences, so when the gaps matter, use Line - Multiple.
      • Only the top three items in the last period are colored orange, pink-red and blue (the line chart's color rule); the rest are gray.
      • Keep to about eight items. More, and the lines tangle until they cannot be read, so keep only the top items.

      Rank Lines

      bump-race

      A card that ranks the items in every period, joins the ranks with S-curves, and plays through the periods one at a time so the moments the order changes are visible. Use it to show how products, channels or branches moved up and down over a year, above all how far one item climbed. Ranks hide the size of the differences, so when the gaps matter, use a line chart.

      Judgment built in (5)
      • Put the period names in labels and 2 to 8 items in items, each with the value of every period (units sold and so on) in values. The card ranks each period from the largest value (better: down makes the smallest value rank 1). Ties follow the previous period's rank, then the listed order, and a null value takes the item out of that period's ranking and breaks its line. Draw 13 months for a monthly trend.
      • Name the item to follow in focus (empty means the first item). Only that item takes the first data color; the rest stay gray, so one path can be followed while the others cross. The label at the end of each line gives the current rank and the name, in place of a legend. Click a line or a label (on the keyboard, pick it with the up and down arrow keys and press Enter) to make that item the one in color, so any item's path can be followed the same way.
      • Rank 1 is at the top and periods are joined with S-curves. Labels ride on the right of the line ends and move with them, so the chart keeps room on the right for the longest label. The scrubber at the bottom is the time axis, so there is no separate period axis: the current period sits under its thumb, with the first and last periods at its ends.
      • When the card comes into view it waits on the first period, then moves on about every half second while the lines grow and the labels move up and down to that period's ranks, so each overtake is visible. It stops on the last period, and the still frame reads as an ordinary bump chart. Rewind, play/pause and fast-forward buttons sit to the right of the scrubber, under the label column. Play starts where the card stopped (from the start when at the end); rewind and fast-forward move one period per click, or keep moving at three times the playing speed while held and stop on the nearest period on release. Drag or click the scrubber, or press the left and right arrow keys, to move one period at a time. With reduced motion turned on, the card draws the last period without playing, and when the data changes and the card redraws, it draws the picked period at once.
      • Hover a line, or focus the chart and press the up and down arrow keys, to see that period's rank, value and move against the previous period while the other items fade. Playback pauses while the pointer is over the chart or the chart has keyboard focus.
