      Cards/Controls

      Controls

      2 cardsHeight 1 unit

      Controls set the period and the ranges of a screen. They send a vzb-change event when the value changes, and a chart card draws again when the JSON inside it changes.

      Range Slider

      control-range

      A control that picks a numeric range with two thumbs. Use it to filter a screen by a continuous value such as an amount or a quantity. The current values always sit in bubbles above the thumbs, and when the thumbs come close the bubbles merge into one range.

      Judgment built in (4)
      • Pick between min and max in steps of step (empty splits the range into about 100 steps). Give the first range in value as [start, end]; empty is the whole range. Values are written by format.
      • The bubbles above the thumbs always show the current values. When the two bubbles come close enough to overlap, they merge into one "start – end" bubble, so the text never overlaps. The two ends that can be picked are written under the track.
      • Each time the value changes, the card sends a vzb-change event. detail.name is name and detail.value is [start, end] as numbers. The current value is also on the card as data-vzb-value.
      • Drag a thumb, press the track to bring the nearer thumb there, or focus a thumb and press the arrow keys to move one step (Page Up and Down about ten steps, Home and End to the end). The thumbs follow the value like a spring and the bubbles lean into the move. With reduced motion turned on, they move at once.

      Date Slider

      control-date

      A control that picks a period with two thumbs. Use it as the date parameter of a whole screen. One step is a month or a day, and putting both thumbs on the same step picks that one month (or day).

      Judgment built in (4)
      • Write min and max as YYYY-MM for one month per step, or as YYYY-MM-DD for one day. Give the first period in value as [start, end] in the same form; empty is the whole period.
      • The bubbles are written short, like Jun '26, and when they come close they merge into one "Jun '26 – Nov '26" bubble. The first and last steps that can be picked are written under the track.
      • Each time the value changes, the card sends a vzb-change event. detail.name is name and detail.value is [start, end] as text in the same form as min; the end is part of the period. The current value is also on the card as data-vzb-value.
      • Drag a thumb, press the track to bring the nearer thumb there, or focus a thumb and press the arrow keys to move one step (Page Up and Down about ten steps, Home and End to the end). The thumbs follow the value like a spring and the bubbles lean into the move. With reduced motion turned on, they move at once.
