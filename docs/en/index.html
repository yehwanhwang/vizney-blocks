    Pick by purpose

    Decide what to show first. Each purpose has a base card; pick another card only when its condition fits.

    1. StatusWhat is the number now?

      BaseKPI - NumberJust the number and its baseline
      • With the last 13 months as a trendKPI - Line
      • To compare the month in progress with past monthsKPI - Bar
      • A total and its breakdown on one cardKPI - Stacked Bar
      • 2–3 metrics with different units on one cardLine - Tabs

    2. TrendHow has it changed?

      BaseLine - BasicThe course of one metric
      • Size per period rather than the courseBar - Basic
      • Against the same month last yearLine - Compare
      • The course of 2–3 items togetherLine - Multiple
      • How rankings changed rather than valuesBump - Basic

    3. TargetHow far to the target?

      BaseKPI - GaugeOne metric against its target or limit
      • Attainment per item, with the amountsGrid - Target
      • A different target in each periodBar - Target
      • This month's cumulative paceLine - Cumulative
      • Whether a baseline or average was crossedLine - Reference

    4. CompareWhich is bigger or smaller?

      BaseBar - RankingSize and rank per item
      • Against last year or last monthBar - Compare
      • Two groups on the same itemsBar - Butterfly
      • Strengths and weaknesses across several scoresRadar - Basic

    5. CompositionWhat is it made of?

      BaseDonut - BasicShares at one point in time
      • How the mix changed over periodsBar - Stacked 100%
      • Total and mix over time, togetherBar - Stacked
      • Many items, or items inside groupsTreemap - Basic
      • Shares you can count, in squaresWaffle - Basic

    6. TableExactly how much, per item?

      BaseGrid - BasicSeveral metrics and a total per item
      • A 13-month trend per item at a glanceGrid - Spark
      • Amounts split two ways, as a crosstabGrid - Pivot
      • Finding high and low cells by colorHeatmap - Basic

    7. ChangeWhy did it go up or down?

      BaseWaterfall - BasicThe change between two points, by factor
      • A profit and loss structureWaterfall - Subtotal
      • Change or variance from target, per periodBar - Diverging

    8. FlowWhere does it drop off, and where does it go?

      BaseFunnel - FlowHow much drops out at each step
      • Flows between two breakdownsSankey - Basic
      • Flows across three or more levelsSankey - Levels

    9. RelationHow do two values relate?

      BaseScatter - BasicWhether two measures move together
      • Sorting items into four groups by two criteriaScatter - Quadrant
      • How two metrics moved over timeScatter - Connected

    How to use

    Nothing to install. One card is an HTML snippet that carries its own styles, data and script.

    1. 1

      Copy and paste

      Press Copy on a card and paste it anywhere inside the <body> of your HTML.

      <div class="vzb-card"
     data-vzb="bar-basic">
  …
</div>
    2. 2

      Change only the JSON

      Edit the values in the card’s JSON. The card computes changes, shares and attainment.

      "title": "Orders by Quarter",
"format": "count",
"labels": ["1Q26", "2Q26", "3Q26"],
"values": [2561, 2654, 2750]
    3. 3

      Tune with variables

      Set colors and the font with :root variables; they apply to every card at once. The list is in tokens.css.

      :root {
  --vzb-primary: #0f766e;
  --vzb-font: 'Pretendard';
}
    4. 4

      Hand it to an AI

      Give a chat AI the card’s AI link; install the skill in a coding agent.

  yehwanhwang/vizney-blocks
    Common fields and layout
    title
    Metric name
    subtitle
    Period subtitle: what is measured, and as of when.
    value
    The headline figure of a KPI card. KPI - Line and KPI - Bar use the last value when it is omitted.
    format
    usd dollar amounts, shortened to $745.6K · $1.2M; count a count (its unit in unit); pct a percentage (changes in pp); days a number of days; eok and krw are Korean won notations
    better
    up, down, neutral. Decides whether a change is colored green or red.
    compare
    { "label", "value" }. The baseline a KPI card computes its change against.
    labels
    Period names, the same length as values; use null for a period without a value.
    series
    [{ "name", "values" }], for several lines or groups of bars.
    rows
    Rows of a table or list, each with a name and its values. The card computes the total row, shares, change rates and attainment.
    curve
    Line shape: smooth (default), linear, step. Used by the line cards and KPI - Line.
    lang
    en makes the words the card prints itself English (Total, Other, Met…). Omit it for Korean.

    Several cards in a row

    Cards do not lay themselves out. Give the wrapper a grid and the rows wrap to the width. Keep each card at least 340px wide; narrower, the text in tables and donuts clips. Cards in a row stretch to the tallest one, so a row of equal-height cards leaves no gaps.

    <div style="display:grid;
  grid-template-columns:
    repeat(auto-fit,
      minmax(min(100%, 340px), 1fr));
  gap:16px;">
  <!-- several cards -->
</div>
