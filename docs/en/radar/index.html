    Radar Chart

    1 cardsHeight 2 units

    Several scores joined into one polygon, to show strengths and weaknesses at a glance. Axes with different units are rescaled to a shared range such as 0–100.

    Chart guide · Radar chart

    Radar - Basic

    radar-basic

    A card that overlays one subject's scores per axis on a baseline. Use it to see where one subject is stronger and weaker than the average, like a dealer review.

    Judgment built in (3)
    • The baseline is marked in series with "base": true. It is laid underneath as a light gray area with a gray outline, and the subject is overlaid as a light orange area with an orange outline. No dots at the vertices.
    • When the card is wide enough, a list on the right gives each axis's score and a badge against the baseline; when narrow, the score sits beside the vertex name. The tooltip gives the difference from the baseline.
    • Keep five to eight axes. The scale's end is set with max; left empty, it is a round number that wraps the values.
