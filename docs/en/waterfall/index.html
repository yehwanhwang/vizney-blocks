    Cards/Waterfall Chart

    Waterfall Chart

    2 cardsHeight 2 units

    How a starting value reaches an ending value, one change at a time. Totals grow from zero and changes float from the previous running total.

    Chart guide · Waterfall chart

    Waterfall - Basic

    waterfall-basic

    A card that splits the difference between two points into changes by factor. Use it to explain why operating profit or revenue changed.

    Judgment built in (3)
    • Write items in order: the starting total, the changes by factor, the ending total. For the ending total, write only "type": "total" and it is filled from the running total.
    • Increasing factors are green and decreasing factors red (the same colors as the KPI badge); totals are orange. For a metric where lower is better, flip the colors with "better": "down".
    • The vertical axis starts at zero. When the changes are too small next to the totals for their bars to show, draw only the changes with Bar - Diverging.

    Waterfall - Subtotal

    waterfall-subtotal

    A card that deducts items in turn from a large value, raising subtotals along the way. Use it to show a profit and loss structure from revenue to operating profit.

    Judgment built in (3)
    • A total without a value ("type": "total") is the running total so far, so subtotals like gross profit and operating profit come out right on their own.
    • Totals and subtotals are orange, deducted items gray, and added items light orange. Costs are part of the structure, not bad values, so red is not used.
    • Change bars are rounded only at the end that moved, and a deducted item's value is written under its bar.
